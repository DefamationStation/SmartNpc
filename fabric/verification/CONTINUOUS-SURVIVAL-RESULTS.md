## Individual progress and current readiness

cooked output and spawned fishing loot produce verified work events. Individual
progress is versioned and bounded separately from Minecraft XP and world
difficulty. Arbitrary support placement does not earn blueprint building credit.
Repeated proof is deduplicated within a simulation-day allowance; history,
known crafts and conservative migration survive entity saves. The entity save
hook flushes a successful action even immediately before shutdown.

carried food, tool tier and remaining durability, owned home and transport
requirements. A golden pickaxe does not count as a stone-capable pickaxe.
Readiness advice does not promise a safe route or grant items. Existing native
behaviours are not newly locked behind an experience level.

## Faster verification

Warm builds freeze each exact jar, build log and unit XML before runtime tests.
The headless runner freezes auxiliary fixture sources and can copy a closed
failure with its actor ledger. A free-space preflight protects save headroom.
Immutable dependencies are first copied and hashed into a private content store;
only private test placements share those bytes. Original files are never linked.
Worlds, configuration, options and logs remain independent writable copies.
The store's 22 tests include process races, corruption and atomic failures.

Preserve failed trials and distinguish helper/fixture errors from production
failures. Low-space or failed-save trials cannot certify persistence. Accelerated
simulation timings are functional evidence, not a controlled FPS benchmark.
